## Sales Pipeline

1. **Portfolio site** — prospects visit our business website to see live examples of our work
2. **Outreach** — cold-calling from a curated spreadsheet (Canadian businesses only, for now)
3. **Access code** — if a prospect takes our call, they receive a unique code
4. **Preview unlock** — the code grants access to a tailored landing page preview matching their business
5. **Deposit** — 30% non-refundable deposit to lock in the project
6. **Final payment** — remaining balance due upon website completion and delivery

**Tooling:** the developer dashboard tracks **viewer analytics** for each
client. Every time a prospect enters their access code, the open is logged —
the dashboard shows total opens, first/last open, and a full open timeline
("viewed 2h ago · 3×"), so follow-up calls are timed off real interest.

## Pricing Models

### Model 1: Flat Buy-Out

The client pays in full and receives everything — the complete codebase, files, folders, secrets, and total ownership. Once transferred, the site is theirs. We can optionally provide ongoing management for a separate fee.

### Model 2: Renting

The website stays under GES ownership. The client pays a recurring fee to use it, but does not receive access to any code, files, or infrastructure. If they cancel, we retain the right to take the site down.

**Important distinction from leasing:** Rent payments do not accumulate toward a buy-out. If a renting client decides to purchase the site outright, they pay the full buy-out price — prior rent does not discount it.
